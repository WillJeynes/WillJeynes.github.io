The People's Messenger is a puzzle game based on the [2 doors and 2 guards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grOpvXBmTx8) riddle. Using an expandable level system, and a JSON-based dialogue tree, we were able to create over a dozen levels building up to and then expanding upon the classic thought experiment. Working part time over the course of a week we managed to place 132nd out of over 2000 entries on our interpretation of the theme.
